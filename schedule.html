Selection Process

100-Point Shortlisting Rubric

Our team reviews every application thoroughly. Beginners are welcome — optional links are scored only when supplied and lack of previous experience will not automatically disqualify you.

30 pts

Motivation & Learning Goal

Clarity on what you want to achieve or build in these 5 days. We value enthusiasm, curiosity, and purposeful dedication.

30 pts

Project / Portfolio Evidence

Any previous demo, design file, code repository, or project description. Evaluated fairly based on your background level.

20 pts

LinkedIn & Profile Presentation

A well-maintained professional profile or online portfolio that helps the review committee understand your journey.

20 pts

Commitment to Daily Tasks

Willingness to attend daily sessions, complete assignments, and actively participate in the final capstone showcase.
