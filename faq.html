Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 5-day bootcamp format, tools covered, pricing, language batches, and post-program opportunities.

It is an intensive, five-day practical bootcamp designed for students, beginner freelancers, creators, early founders, and developers. Unlike traditional theoretical lectures, participants use selected AI tools to build web experiences, app prototypes, visual assets, AI workflows, and early startup MVPs with daily guided assistance.

The program is specifically tailored for:
• College students, student innovators, and campus project builders.
• Beginner freelancers and independent creators exploring AI services.
• Early-stage startup founders validating new digital products.
• AI enthusiasts, software developers, UI/UX designers, and working professionals.

We offer three independent cohorts: Tamil (தமிழ்), Hindi (हिन्दी), and English. Each cohort has its own dedicated schedule, IST time slot, trainers, and session links. When applying, you choose one primary language track. We do not run all three languages simultaneously in a single session.

No advanced prior experience is required. Day 1 starts with foundational prompt design and tool literacy before progressing into visual generation, no-code workflows, and AI-assisted building. If you can use a web browser, you have the foundational skills needed.

All you need is a laptop or desktop computer with internet access and a personal Google account. We prioritize free-tier tools and workflows wherever practical. Any required tool links and instructions will be provided in advance.

The standard fee is ₹1,000. All applications undergo review, and up to 100 participants are shortlisted for a 50% discount (paying only ₹500). Selection is based on our transparent 100-point rubric assessing your learning goals, profile, and enthusiasm.

To receive a verified certificate jointly recognized by the organizers, you must attend the live sessions and complete the guided daily build assignments leading up to the Day 5 showcase demo.

Every qualifying participant receives a voucher for 50% off an eligible upcoming internship organized by Mecollabx and Buildrix. Furthermore, top performers demonstrating exceptional execution may be evaluated for direct internship or freelance project roles with the organizing teams.

Once you submit your application, all updates regarding shortlisting, payment links, and cohort onboarding links will be sent directly via your provided email address and WhatsApp number.
