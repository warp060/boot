Hands-on 5-Day Plan

From Tool Literacy to
Building & Launching

This is an intensive practical bootcamp, not a lecture-only event. Each day moves you progressively from hands-on AI tool fundamentals to guided builds, workflow automation, and portfolio-ready startup prototypes.

Day-by-Day Agenda

The 5-Day Learning Path

Participants build towards at least one guided prototype or project artefact and learn how to present, improve, and package their skills.

01
AI Ecosystem
Gemini, AI Studio, structured prompt design, multimodal AI (text, code, image, video understanding), responsible AI principles, and choosing the right tool for the job.
Day 1 Output
Curated Prompt Collection & AI-Assisted Task Automation
02
AI-Assisted App & Web Building
Google Antigravity (subject to availability), AI pair programming, crafting responsive web applications, animated modern websites, component UI/UX patterns, and rapid interactive prototyping.
Day 2 Output
Functional Interactive Web or Mobile App Prototype
03
Generative Media & Storytelling
Eligible image and video generation tools, visual storytelling frameworks, marketing design assets, brand identity concepts, and product-promotional social media content creation.
Day 3 Output
Complete Visual Brand Campaign or Promo Video Concept
04
AI Workflows & Autonomous Agents
Google Opal and other eligible low-code/no-code platforms, API integrations, autonomous task automation, custom AI assistants, and enterprise agentic use cases.
Day 4 Output
Working Mini AI Workflow or Custom Conversational Assistant
05
Freelancing & Startup Building
Problem discovery, MVP thinking, converting prototypes into client-ready landing pages, live demos, portfolio packaging, freelance service pricing, and client pitching.
Day 5 Output
Final Project Demo Showcase & Next-Step Career/Startup Roadmap

Ideation & Research
